How to play Rooftop Drive

Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to steer. Press Space or Up to jump over roof gaps. Hold Shift or the boost button to spend stored charge for a speed boost. Collect cyan energy cells to refill boost charge. Avoid road barriers, collect energy, and travel as far as you can. Press Escape or P to pause.
